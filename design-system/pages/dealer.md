# Dealer profile (`/dealer/<key>/`): overrides

- **Header:** dark ink panel with initials or logo tile (cream border), business name in Literata, white "Visit website" button.
- **Claim box:** gold-tint callout with a gold "Claim this profile" button. This is the one gold button on the page.
- **Next show card:** gold-tint, 1.5px `--brass` border, radius 10px, large green table number.
- **"Add their shows to my calendar":** ghost `.btn-sm` that opens a white popup (`.cal-pop`, `--shadow-pop`). The popup holds a green "iPhone, Mac or Outlook" button, a ghost "Google Calendar" button and a text link to download the file. It is a calendar subscription, so it explains itself in one line, unlike the show page's single-event menu.
